Decisions agreed by Pushkal Kapoor, 14 Oct 2026. The six design decisions below are approved and built into every screen of this blueprint. Business values marked as placeholders are still open.
Agreed · Fee model

Free account, then a paid access pass

The Aug 2024 ISF announcement says faculty "can register free, view the standards and access learning materials". The brief now says ₹2,500 + GST for basic access. Agreed: registration is free; the ₹2,500 + GST access pass unlocks the standard, library and self-evaluation. Pass validity still to confirm.

Agreed · AI detection

Never auto-fail on an AI score

AI-text detectors have documented false-positive rates, and they are worse for non-native English writers, which is much of this cohort. Showing candidates a live "AI detected" meter also teaches them to paraphrase until it goes green. Blueprint design: paste blocked, typing telemetry captured, originality status shown to the candidate in plain terms, the numeric score visible only to assessors, and a human decision on every flag.

Agreed · Eligibility before payment

Check credentials before taking the certification fee

The brief has payment first, then admin review of credentials. That guarantees refunds and disputes when someone pays for Leadership and is only eligible for Basic. Blueprint flow: credentials → automatic pre-check → admin decision (approve / offer lower level / reject) → then payment.

Agreed · SEO

A subdomain of helms.in will not rank on its own

The current site at cadcf.helms.in inherits HELMS's domain and has almost no indexable text. "Runs independently with its own SEO" needs its own domain (for example a cadcf domain), server-rendered public pages, and HELMS reduced to optional single sign-on.

Agreed · Content security

Private YouTube links leak

"Private" YouTube videos need each viewer's Google account invited; "unlisted" ones can be forwarded to anyone. Neither suits a paid library. Use a domain-locked host (Vimeo, Bunny Stream, Mux) with signed URLs. The same applies to the standard: view-only with watermark deters sharing, but no web viewer can stop a screenshot.

Agreed · Scope

Project assignment missing from the brief

The published process lists objective and subjective assessment, project assignments, oral examination and live teaching assessment. The brief omits the project assignment. The blueprint keeps it as an optional stage, switchable per level in Admin › Settings.

Persona 1

Faculty candidate

Maritime trainer, instructor or lecturer, often at sea earlier in career, many outside India.

  • Needs to know which level they can realistically attempt
  • Pays in INR or USD; wants a proper invoice
  • Works across time zones; books slots in their local time
  • Writes in English as a second language for many
Persona 2

ISF admin team

Runs the scheme: eligibility, scheduling, proctoring, assessor allocation, results, payments, content.

  • Needs one queue showing what is waiting on ISF
  • Must schedule Zoom-proctored sessions and invigilators
  • Issues certificates only after moderation
  • Answers to ClassNK audits, so needs a full audit trail
Persona 3

Assessor / panellist

Senior faculty and industry experts marking scripts, reviewing lectures and sitting on interview panels.

  • Marks against the standard's criteria, not opinion
  • Sees originality evidence, decides on it
  • Declares conflicts of interest before any allocation
  • Should see only what they are allocated (blind marking where possible)

Frequently asked

Can I download the standard?

No. Registered members can read the complete standard online. Each page carries your name and member ID.

What if I apply for a level I am not eligible for?

ISF reviews your credentials before you pay the certification fee. If another level fits better, we will offer it to you.

How is the written test supervised?

You join a Zoom call, share your screen and keep your camera on. An ISF invigilator watches the session live.

Can I use AI tools to write my answers?

No. Answers must be your own writing. Pasting is disabled and every subjective answer goes through an originality check. Flags are reviewed by a human assessor, who may ask you about your answer in the interview.

How does someone check my certificate?

Each certificate has a QR code and number that open a public verification page.

5. Notifications

  • Access pass purchased → receipt + GST invoice
  • Application submitted → candidate + ISF admin
  • Eligibility decision / offer / info request → candidate
  • Payment captured or failed → candidate; captured → admin
  • Session booked; reminder 24 h and 30 min with Zoom link
  • Allocation → assessor, with due date; overdue reminder
  • Result per stage → candidate after moderation
  • Certificate issued; expiry reminder 90 and 30 days out

6. Non-functional

  • Times stored in UTC, shown in each user's time zone
  • 2FA mandatory for staff and assessors
  • Exam autosave every 15 s; resume after disconnect
  • Works on 2 Mbps connections; lecture upload resumable
  • Data protection: India DPDP Act 2023; GDPR for EU candidates
  • Retention policy for recordings and scripts Set with ClassNK requirements
  • WCAG 2.1 AA for public and candidate pages
